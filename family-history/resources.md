These are the databases and tools I use most frequently in my research. All external links open in new tabs.

## Genealogy Databases

- [Ancestry](https://www.ancestry.co.uk){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — census records, birth/marriage/death certificates, immigration records
- [FindMyPast](https://www.findmypast.co.uk){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — strong UK and Ireland coverage, newspaper archives
- [FamilySearch](https://www.familysearch.org){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — free access, wide international coverage
- [Jewishgen](https://www.jewishgen.org){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — specialist database for Jewish genealogy research worldwide

## Collaborative Family Trees

- [WikiTree — Callender-45](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Callender-45){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — my public WikiTree profile; contributions and corrections welcome

## Software

- [Gramps](https://gramps-project.org){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — open-source genealogy software used for my personal database

## Archives

- [The National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — UK government records
- [The British Newspaper Archive](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — historical newspaper digitisation project
- [Commonwealth War Graves Commission](https://www.cwgc.org){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} — records for those who died in both world wars
